How this page works

Player pool: the November 2026 Nations League roster plus regulars from the 2026 World Cup and the September–October camp who are under 40 and currently playing professionally. Caps and international goals come from U.S. Soccer roster releases. Club stats and ratings come from FotMob player pages; xG is FotMob's expected-goals model. FotMob ratings run from 0 to 10, with roughly 7.0 meaning a solid match. A dash means the stat hasn't been collected yet; the scheduled refresh fills those in.
